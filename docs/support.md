## Compatibility

The iOS apps are iPhone apps and require iOS 26.0 or later.

Glasshole supports Apple Intelligence on compatible iPhone models. Other
supported iPhones can use configured cloud, local-network, PocketPal, or
custom providers. Food Scanner's default Local iPhone Vision mode works on
supported iPhones without an API key.

## Food Scanner

Food Scanner can be used without companion hardware by choosing a food photo
from iPhone or taking a new iPhone camera photo. The optional Google Glass XE
companion APK can capture a food image and send it to the iOS app over local
Wi-Fi or iPhone Personal Hotspot.

On Glass, open the **scan food** command and **tap** the touchpad to launch
and capture. Do not rely on saying “Ok Glass.”

Nutrition values are AI estimates for general information only, not medical
advice.

## Glasshole

Glasshole can be used without companion hardware by tapping the microphone
button in the iOS app. The optional Google Glass XE companion APK can start and
stop real-time conversation capture and display AI-powered fact-check overlays
after analysis.

On Glass, open the **fact check** command and **tap** the touchpad to launch,
then tap again to start/stop listening. Do not rely on saying “Ok Glass.”

Use Glasshole only with the consent of everyone being recorded and in
compliance with local laws and regulations where you use it.

## Common Setup (website → GitHub)

1. Install the iOS app from the App Store
   ([Food Scanner](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/food-scanner-ai-overlay/id6790629113) or
   [Glasshole AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glasshole-ai-for-google-glass/id6784266697)).
2. From the product page on
   [scalinghuman.ai](https://scalinghuman.ai) (or the links below), download the
   matching companion APK:
   - [Food Scanner companion](https://github.com/scalinghuman/experiments/releases/download/glass-companions-v1.0.0/foodscanner-glass-companion-v1.0.0.apk)
   - [Glasshole AI companion](https://github.com/scalinghuman/experiments/releases/download/glass-companions-v1.0.0/glasshole-glass-companion-v1.0.0.apk)
3. Follow the
   [Google Glass XE installation guide](https://github.com/scalinghuman/experiments/blob/main/docs/glass/GLASS_INSTALL_GUIDE.md)
   for ADB install, USB debugging, and hotspot setup.
4. Connect Google Glass XE to your iPhone Personal Hotspot, open the iPhone
   app, and confirm the connection indicator.
5. On Glass, **tap** the **fact check** or **scan food** command — do not
   depend on “Ok Glass” voice wake.
